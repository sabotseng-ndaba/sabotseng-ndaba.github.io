1. Agile Team Charter

Evidence

Our team created an Agile Team Charter for BrightStart English Tutoring, an educational website intended to support English literacy for Grade 1–3 learners in South Africa.

The charter explained our project vision, team roles, communication methods, working agreements, decision-making process, Definition of Done, team values, risks and project constraints. We used Trello to organise tasks and monitor progress, with Microsoft Teams, WhatsApp and email for communication.

My role was Product Owner. My responsibilities included defining the business idea, setting project goals, prioritising tasks and helping ensure that the project met its requirements.

Personal Reflection

The Agile Team Charter helped our BrightStart English Tutoring team understand the purpose of the project, our individual roles and how we were expected to work together. It established our working agreements, communication methods and the use of Trello to manage tasks and monitor progress.

As the Product Owner, I was responsible for defining the business idea, setting project goals, prioritising tasks and ensuring that the project met its requirements. I contributed by coordinating presentations, organising backlog and sprint tasks, following up with team members and helping move the project forward.

However, our experience did not always match the working agreements in the charter. We experienced inconsistent participation, communication difficulties, internet connectivity problems and delays in preparing for presentations. This showed me that having agreed rules is not enough; team members must follow them consistently, and responsibilities must be clearly communicated.

I learned that an Agile Team Charter provides direction and accountability, but it must also be reviewed and applied throughout the project. In future projects, I would encourage more regular check-ins, clearer task ownership and earlier reporting of blockers. This would help the team identify problems sooner and work more effectively towards its goals.
