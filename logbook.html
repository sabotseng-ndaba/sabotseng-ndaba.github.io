PERSONAL REFLECTION

What the logbook taught me

Keeping a logbook helped me see how much coordination happens outside the actual coding work. I followed up on presentations, Trello updates, task allocation, repository access, testing and deployment.

It also showed me the importance of recording work regularly. Some notes covered a few days rather than one day, and some dates had no complete record. This made it harder to reconstruct the timeline later.

In future projects, I would update my logbook after each work session, record decisions and blockers clearly, and connect entries to relevant evidence. This would make my contribution easier to track and help the team respond to problems sooner.
