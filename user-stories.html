PERSONAL REFLECTION

What I learned

Organising user stories and tasks helped me understand that a project should focus on what users need and why those needs matter. Tasks then break the work into actions the team can plan and complete.

As Product Owner, I helped coordinate project work, communicate outstanding tasks and organise the Trello board. One area that needed improvement was making sure stories and tasks were recorded consistently across the sprints.

I learned that user stories should be clear enough for the team to understand the expected outcome. In future projects, I would review stories with the team, check that each one has clear acceptance criteria where appropriate, and make sure related tasks are assigned and tracked.
